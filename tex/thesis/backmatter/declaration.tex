\chapter*{Declaration of Authorship}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Declaration of Authorship}
\label{chap:declaration}

I declare that I wrote this thesis independently, that I used no sources or aids other
than those stated, and that all passages taken from other works, whether verbatim or in
substance, are identified as such. The drawings, figures, and tables in this thesis were
created by me or are provided with a corresponding source reference. The use of
generative artificial intelligence is declared separately and in full in the Declaration
on the Use of Artificial Intelligence (page~\pageref{chap:ai-usage}). This thesis has not
previously been submitted, in the same or a similar form, to any examination authority.
