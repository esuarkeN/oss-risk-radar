\chapter*{Declaration on the Use of Artificial Intelligence}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Declaration on the Use of Artificial Intelligence}
\label{chap:ai-usage}

Generative artificial intelligence was used during the development of the software
artifact and during the preparation of this thesis. This declaration records that use
in a form that can be independently verified.

The assistant used was \emph{Claude Opus 4.8} (Anthropic), operated through the Claude
Code command-line interface. It was used for software implementation, refactoring, test
authoring, debugging, data analysis, and for drafting and revising prose in this
document. Every contribution was reviewed, corrected where necessary, and accepted by the
author, who takes full responsibility for the content of this thesis and for the
correctness of the artifact.

Two categories of assistance are distinguished. Contributions that entered the version
control history of the artifact are recorded as commit trailers of the form
\texttt{Co\sdash Authored\sdash By: Claude Opus 4.8}, and are enumerated in
Table~\ref{tab:ai-commits}. The complete and current list can be regenerated from the
repository at any time with:

\begin{verbatim}
git log --grep="Co-Authored-By: Claude" --format="%h %ad %s" --date=short
\end{verbatim}

Contributions that did not produce a commit --- interactive discussion, exploratory
analysis, and drafting of text that was subsequently rewritten by the author --- are not
individually enumerable and are declared here collectively.

No generative model was used to produce, synthesize, or alter any empirical result. All
figures reported in this thesis originate from the training and evaluation pipeline
described in Chapter~\ref{chap:implementation}, are reproducible from the recorded dataset
hash (Appendix~\ref{sec:method-reproducibility}), and were not authored by a language
model. No bibliographic reference in this thesis was generated by a language model; every
entry was verified against its source.

At the time of writing, \(16\) of the \(83\) commits in the repository carry the
co-authorship trailer.
